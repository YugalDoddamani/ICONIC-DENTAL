Ultrasonic cleaning, polishing and oral checkup

Brushing misses hardened tartar along the gumline and stains from coffee, tea and food. Ultrasonic scaling lifts it away with gentle vibration instead of scraping the enamel. One visit and your teeth feel noticeably smoother.

02 — Treatments

Ten treatments, one diagnosis first

Every treatment is planned around your comfort. If you are not sure which one you need, book a consultation and we will look at it together.

Ultrasonic Dental Cleaning

Scaling and polishing that removes tartar, plaque and surface stains without touching the enamel.

Microscopic Root Canal

Root canal therapy performed under a surgical microscope. Most cases finish in a single sitting.

Braces and Clear Aligners

Metal, ceramic and invisible aligner options for crowded teeth and bite correction.

Smile Makeovers and Veneers

Porcelain veneers, whitening and bonding planned around your facial proportions.

Dental Implants

Titanium tooth replacement for single or full mouth restoration.

Crowns, Bridges and Dentures

Zirconia and all-ceramic restorations matched to your natural tooth shade.

Tooth Restoration

Tooth-colored composite fillings for chipped, fractured or decayed teeth.

Kids Dentistry

Fluoride therapy, sealants and gentle checkups for children.

Tooth and Wisdom Removal

Impacted wisdom tooth and routine removal under local anesthesia.

Facial Aesthetics and Wellness

Aesthetic consultations and nutrition guidance for long-term oral health.
